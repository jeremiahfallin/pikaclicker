## 2025-04-28 - Dynamic confirmation text on destructive actions
**Learning:** Users need clear explicit text and accessible labels indicating destructive actions. Relying only on a secondary click on the same 'Release' button is a poor UX.
**Action:** Ensure destructive action buttons update their visible text and `aria-label` to explicitly state the action being confirmed (e.g., 'Confirm Release') rather than just showing identical text or changing button color.
