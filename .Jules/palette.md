## 2024-05-31 - Explicit confirmation for destructive actions
**Learning:** Relying solely on color shifts (like changing a button to red) for destructive action confirmation isn't sufficiently accessible or clear.
**Action:** Always update the visible text (e.g., "Confirm release") and provide an explicit `aria-label` detailing the target (e.g., "Confirm release of [Item]") to ensure clarity for all users.
