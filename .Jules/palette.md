## 2024-05-17 - Destructive Action Confirmation
**Learning:** Relying purely on a secondary click with unchanged button text for destructive actions (like releasing a Pokémon) is confusing and inaccessible.
**Action:** Always update the visible text and aria-label of the confirmation button to explicitly state the action being confirmed (e.g., 'Confirm release of [Item]') to ensure clarity and accessibility.
